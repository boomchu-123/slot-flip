This download is free, and must stay free.

Two of the emulator cores inside it forbid commercial use:

  - snes9x2005 (System/snes9x2005_plus_libretro.so) is a Snes9x derivative. Its
    licence (snes9x2005-copyright.txt, in this folder) allows use, copying,
    modification and distribution for NON-COMMERCIAL purposes only, provided that
    notice goes with every copy. In its words, commercial use includes charging
    money for it or for software derived from it, including in commercial game
    bundles, and using it as a promotion for a commercial product.
  - Genesis Plus GX (System/genesis_plus_gx_libretro.so) is released under a licence
    (genesis_plus_gx-LICENSE.txt, in this folder) that says redistributions may not
    be sold, nor be used in a commercial product or activity, and must reproduce its
    copyright notice. Its source is in genesis_plus_gx-src.tar.gz.

So, for this whole download (Slot.pak):
  - You may use it, copy it and share it, for free.
  - Do not sell it, charge for it, or bundle it with anything that is sold.
  - Keep this folder, with its licence texts, whenever you share it.

The rest of slot-flip is free software under its own licences (the frontend is
GPL-3.0-or-later; mGBA is MPL-2.0; gpSP, PCSX-ReARMed and fceumm are GPL-2.0). Their
texts are in this folder too. The cores are separate programs the frontend loads at
run time, not part of it.

If you want a build for sale, remove System/snes9x2005_plus_libretro.so and the files
named snes9x2005* in this folder, and System/genesis_plus_gx_libretro.so and the files
named genesis_plus_gx* in this folder; the rest has no such restriction. (This is a plain
reading of the licences, not legal advice.)
